## 60-second demo clip — shot-by-shot script

Goal: a first-time viewer *gets it* in 60s. Show, don't explain.

**[0:00–0:05] Hook — lead with the moment**
- Screen: live match, overlay already visible.
- Say: "This is a live World Cup match — and I'm about to bet on the next goal
  without ever leaving the stream. Watch."
- ⚠️ Do NOT open with "Hi, I'm Peter, founder of…" — credentials go at the END.

**[0:05–0:20] The problem, in one line**
- Say: "Normally you'd pause, open a betting app, miss the action, bet on stale
  odds. I'm not doing any of that."
- Screen: tap a player / market on the overlay — live odds visible.

**[0:20–0:40] The bet — the whole product**
- Say: "Next goal, [Team]. Five bucks." → place via overlay (or voice if wired).
- Screen: confirmation card → bet locked → "settles instantly in USDC, on-chain."
- Keep eyes on the match the whole time — visual proof you never left the stream.

**[0:40–0:55] The payoff**
- Best case: a goal settles live on camera — CLIP THIS, it's gold.
- Else: "When it hits, it settles instantly — no waiting, no app, no middleman."

**[0:55–1:00] CTA + credibility**
- Say: "I'm a former pro player, ex-Sportradar — we built this for the World Cup.
  Link's pinned, download the extension, bet alongside me next match."
- Screen: download link + "Beta — testnet USDC, real winnings."

**Production notes**
- Film once, for real, during a match. Energy > polish. One good take wins.
- Most valuable 5s = a bet settling on a real goal. Get it → it's your hook clip.
- Make a 15s cutdown (bet + settle only) for X/TikTok.
