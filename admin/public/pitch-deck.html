SCQA

Situation

Live sports in-game betting is already larger than pre-match betting, but existing platforms feel static - placing bets then waiting passively for outcomes.

Complication

Real sports are dynamic and thrilling. Current betting is not - platforms kill the excitement forcing users to leave streams, navigate boring apps, and miss the live action while placing bets.

Question

How do we make betting as dynamic and interactive as the sports themselves?

Answer

We built patent-pending betting overlays that turn any sport stream into an interactive gaming experience - bet on in-game micro-moments in real-time without ever leaving your screen. Any sport. Any device. Any event.

Use Case + Founder Story

Why Me

Former professional football player with deep industry connections across sports data (StatsPerform, Ross, Phenix), live event collection (ex-Sportsradar scout), and Web3 development. I understand the complete ecosystem: how fans think, what drives betting behavior, and the technical infrastructure needed to deliver real-time experiences.

The User Problem

Sports are interactive and thrilling, but betting is static and boring. Fans want to react to developing moments, but current platforms force them to leave streams, miss action, and bet on stale odds.

Why Now

Live betting already exceeds pre-match betting, proving fans want real-time engagement. Polymarket showed onchain prediction markets work ($1B+ volume). Crypto infrastructure now enables instant settlement while overlay technology makes betting part of viewing, not separate from it.

Solution + Differentiation

What We Built

Patent-pending betting overlays turning any sports stream into an interactive gaming experience. Users bet on micro-moments directly inside video players with instant onchain USDC settlement.

Revenue Model

1.5-2% take rate on live bets + fiat on-ramp fees + USDC deposit spreads + token trading fees + validator fees + SportScout oracle licensing. Target ARPA: $540/year.

Why We Win

  • Regulatory counter-positioning vs legacy sportsbooks
  • Exclusive real-time sports data + proprietary SportScout network
  • Interactive gaming experience vs passive betting
  • Instant settlement + transparent onchain infrastructure

Why Competitors Fail

They build better sportsbooks when users want interactive sports experiences. We compete against passive viewing, not only betting apps.
